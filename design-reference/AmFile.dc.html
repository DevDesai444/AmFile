3.2.P.5  Control of Drug Product

3.2.P.5.1 Specification(s)

The drug product specification for CombiPatch (estradiol/norethindrone acetate) transdermal system is presented in Table 3.2.P.5.1-1. Specifications were established in accordance with ICH Q6A decision trees and are supported by batch analysis data from three registration batches manufactured at the Brookhaven facility.

Release and shelf-life acceptance criteria are identical except where noted. The dissolution acceptance criterion of Q = 70% at 24 hours was set on the basis of the pivotal bioequivalence batch, and justification is provided in Section 3.2.P.5.6a justification narrative is provided in Section 3.2.P.5.6.

3.2.P.5.2 Analytical Procedures

Analytical procedures are described in the referenced method documents. Method AM-2204 for related substances has not been cross-referenced to the validation report in 3.2.P.5.3, and the system suitability limits stated here differ from those in the stability protocol.

Residual solvent testing follows USP <467> Option 1. Where a solvent is used only in the adhesive coating step, a risk-based justification per ICH Q3C(R8) is provided
